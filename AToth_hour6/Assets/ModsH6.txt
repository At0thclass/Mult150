A. Add multiple finish zones. See if you can place them in a way that offers the player more choices. For example, a dangerous shortcut route vs an easy but lengthier one.

There are two finish zones. Both require you to pass through a field of lava pits, the second exit requires you to scale a mountain, and avoid some spikes.

B. Modify the level to have more, and different hazards. (At least 2 new hazards that are not water.)

I added lava (source: https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/2d/textures-materials/stylized-lava-materials-180943 ), and spikes (source: https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/ball-pack-446).

C. Modify the game to include an element of verticality in your game to make use of the jumping mechanic! (spacebar to jump)

I added blocks along a mountain range. You have to jump on these blocks in order to access the second exit. You may have to jump over spike balls too

D. Modify the sky and textures to create a world based on your theme.

I made the directional light red, which makes the map look more like you're in a volcanic area.
